Elder Christian C. Chigbundu is a General Authority Seventy. He was born in Nigeria in 1974. He worked hard in his job and also served in many Church callings. He teaches people how to stay strong in hard times.


In his talk he said we need spiritual immunity. This means we stay close to God so hard things do not break us. He said small things like prayer, scriptures, the sacrament, and covenants help us a lot. These simple things give us strength every day.

“As we build our spiritual immunity, we develop the strength and courage to withstand the challenges of life.”
