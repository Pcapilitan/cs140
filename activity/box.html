1. Why removing fixed height is usually better: Removing fixed height allows the container to adjust its height based on the content inside.
2. Why the card remains exactly 400px(box-sizing: border-box): box-sizing: border-box includes the padding and border widths inside the designated 400px total width.
3. What auto is doing in margin: 0 auto: auto automatically divides the remaining horizontal space equally between the left and right margins to center the element.
