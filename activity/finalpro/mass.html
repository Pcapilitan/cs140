What is Simbang Gabi?

[Image Placeholder: Simbang Gabi Dawn Mass]

Simbang Gabi (Night Mass) is a cherished nine-day devotional series of dawn Masses held from December 16 to December 24 in anticipation of Christmas Day. Rooted in Spanish colonial history when farmers attended Mass before working in the fields at sunrise, it remains a pillar of Filipino Catholic faith.

What People Do and What They Bring:

  • Wake Up Early: Families wake up before dawn (around 3:00 AM to 4:00 AM) to walk together to brightly lit churches.
  • Complete All Nine Masses: Devotees strive to attend all nine consecutive mornings, believing that completing the novena grants a special wish.
  • Bring Prayers & Offerings: Churchgoers bring rosaries, prayer books, and love offerings for the parish.
  • Enjoy Traditional Delicacies: After Mass, families gather outside the church to buy and share warm bibingka (rice cake) and puto bumbong (purple sticky rice) paired with hot salabat (ginger tea).
