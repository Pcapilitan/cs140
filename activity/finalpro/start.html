Why Christmas Starts in September in the Philippines

In the Philippines, the Christmas countdown officially begins on September 1st, marking the start of the famous "Ber" months (September, October, November, and December). As soon as the calendar flips to September, shopping malls hang festive garlands, radio stations begin playing classic Jose Mari Chan holiday songs, and families start planning their reunions.

This early celebration reflects the deep cultural importance of family, faith, and optimism in Filipino society. With millions of Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) saving up and preparing balikbayan boxes months in advance, starting early builds excitement and gives everyone ample time to reunite and celebrate together.
