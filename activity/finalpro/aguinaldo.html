What is Aguinaldo and Why Kids Love It

Aguinaldo refers to the Christmas gifts, most commonly crisp peso bills tucked inside colorful envelopes (ampao)—given to children by their godparents (Ninong and Ninang) and older relatives. On Christmas Day, kids dress in their best outfits to visit their godparents, perform the respectful "Mano po" gesture, and joyfully receive their Aguinaldo!
