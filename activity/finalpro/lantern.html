The Ligligan Parul (Giant Lantern Festival) Competition

Held annually every December in the City of San Fernando, Pampanga—known as the "Christmas Capital of the Philippines"—Ligligan Parul is a dazzling competition where different barangays build colossal, 20-foot lanterns illuminated by thousands of dancing bulbs synchronized to festive music.
