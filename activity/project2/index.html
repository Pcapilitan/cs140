Philippine Islands

Welcome to my photo tour website showing five beautiful island destinations in the Philippines!

  • Boracay - Famous for powder-white sand, vibrant beach nightlife, and endless ocean fun.
  • Palawan - Dramatic limestone cliffs and hidden lagoons in El Nido.
  • Panglao - Rich coral reefs and pristine shores in Bohol.
  • Siargao - The surfing capital of the Philippines with legendary swells.
  • Kalanggaman - A stunning sandbar paradise in Leyte.
